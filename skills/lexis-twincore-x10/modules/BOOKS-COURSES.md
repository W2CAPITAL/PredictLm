# BOOKS & COURSES SOURCE GATE

## Goal

Use books, courses and videos as durable learning sources without treating every repository/container as licensed training data.

## Rights order

1. public-domain / explicitly open-licensed work;
2. user-provided material the user is authorized to use;
3. public metadata, table of contents and short source-grounded excerpts;
4. discovery-only catalog;
5. quarantine when rights/provenance are unclear.

A repository-level license does not automatically relicense embedded books or PDFs.

## Books

For a book question:
SOURCE/EDITION → PASSAGE/CHAPTER → RETRIEVE → ANSWER → PROVENANCE.

Separate:
- what the text says;
- interpretation;
- historical/outside context;
- uncertainty/gaps.

Project Gutenberg/GITenberg works require work-by-work rights/edition checks.

## YouTube

Ciência Todo Dia may be used as a complementary science explainer source via public metadata/search.
For disputed, medical, legal, financial or otherwise high-stakes claims, cross-check with primary/academic/official material.
Do not bulk-copy full copyrighted transcripts by default.

## LinkedIn Learning

Use course metadata for discovery and user-authorized notes/transcript excerpts for Tutor/Reading workflows.
Do not bulk-copy or redistribute protected videos, transcripts or exercise files.

## Persistence

Approved notes/lessons can enter the global-learning proposal flow.
Raw copyrighted course/video/book content does not become a global lesson automatically.
