PredictLM adapts high-level workflow patterns:
- inspect the real project before scripting;
- hook-first launch storytelling;
- show real product UI/user flow;
- 15–25 second launch-video target;
- readability floors for on-screen copy;
- storyboard -> composition brief -> render -> verify -> poster -> share copy;
- pre-render validation;
- deliberate poster-frame selection;
- do not leak project secrets into public launch media.

PredictLM-specific changes:
- renderer/provider agnostic;
- no mandatory Hyperframes dependency;
- hosted-provider capability preflight;
- explicit async job verification;
- four-core lightweight media control;
- legal/product claims must stay evidence-bound;
- no completion claim until a playable artifact exists.
