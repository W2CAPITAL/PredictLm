# PredictLM — Commercial Launch Film

> Direction contract: `skills/brag-launch-video/SKILL.md`  
> Creative source pattern: `latent-spaces/brag` (MIT)  
> Render target: 15s · 16:9 · product-first · no voiceover

## Product thesis

PredictLM is a general AI assistant with a legal-first workflow when the user brings a Brazilian court process. The same Chat can route into legal consultation, Build, Work, Tutor, Research and media capabilities without turning each capability into a disconnected product.

## Angle

**One assistant. Verified workflows.**

The commercial should feel like a product film for a serious developer tool: dark graphite UI, precise typography, restrained motion, real product states and zero generic “AI magic” filler.

## Hook — 0.0–2.5s

- Black/graphite background.
- `PREDICTLM` resolves with a short scan/glow.
- Secondary copy: **General AI. Legal-first when it matters.**
- A real-looking Chat surface becomes visible behind the title.
- One restrained low-frequency impact + UI tick.

Acceptance:
- logo/title settled for at least ~0.8s;
- tagline readable for at least ~1.5s;
- no hologram/cyberpunk filler.

## Legal flow — 2.5–6.5s

Show the actual product flow:

```
CNJ
  -> tribunal resolution
  -> DataJud + DJEN
  -> normalized timeline
  -> plain-language answer
```

On-screen copy:

**CNJ → DataJud + DJEN → timeline → clear answer**

UI elements:
- fictional CNJ number;
- DataJud source card;
- DJEN source card;
- three-event timeline;
- final explanation card.

Privacy rule: no real client names, phone numbers, emails, credentials or case data.

## Build / Work — 6.5–10.5s

Transition from Chat into a code/project execution view without opening a separate “product universe”.

Show:
- file tree;
- architecture plan;
- concise diff;
- VERIFY stage;
- checks turning green.

Copy:

**Build · Work · Tutor · Research**

A compact four-core telemetry strip appears:

**Fly · Mouse · Macaque · Human**

Meaning:
- Fly: salience / hook;
- Mouse: visual/spatial discrimination;
- Macaque: visual hierarchy / composition;
- Human: working memory / executive QA.

They are software control signals, not biological-brain imagery.

## Imagine — 10.5–13.0s

A small Imagine card receives a prompt and produces one result.

Show:
- one prompt;
- loading/progress state;
- one image result;
- provider details omitted from the hero film.

The shot exists to establish breadth, not to dominate the commercial.

## Outro — 13.0–15.0s

Centered lockup:

**PREDICTLM**

**One assistant. Verified workflows.**

Footer:

**Chat · Jurídico · Imagine**

End cleanly. No fake benchmark, customer logo, testimonial or unsupported metric.

## Audio

- restrained electronic pulse;
- subtle typing/UI ticks;
- two low transition impacts;
- no voiceover;
- no aggressive trailer riser;
- audio supports motion, never hides product copy.

## Composition brief

- Resolution: 1280×720 minimum; 1920×1080 preferred for final publish.
- Aspect: 16:9.
- FPS: 30.
- Duration: 15 seconds.
- Visual system: graphite/near-black, white text, restrained electric blue/violet accents.
- Typography: geometric sans / developer-tool feel; no novelty display font.
- Motion: fast entrance, readable hold, clean exit.
- Data: entirely fictional.
- Product claims: limited to behavior supported by the repository.
- Poster candidate: settled legal-flow frame around 5s or final lockup around 14s.

## Provider / renderer handoff

The creative plan is renderer-agnostic.

Preferred execution order:
1. configured temporal provider/compositor;
2. configured local/self-hosted compositor;
3. deterministic motion-graphics render with FFmpeg;
4. storyboard only when no renderer exists.

A provider accepting a job is not success. Success requires a playable terminal video asset.
